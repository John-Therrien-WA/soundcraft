# soundcraft, Wild Animal's fork: the DAW's native audio backend

This checkout is Wild Animal's fork of `storytold/soundcraft`, the agent-built Pro Tools clone in Rust (MIT or Apache 2.0, first commit 6 October 2026). Upstream's own agent entry point is `AGENTS.md` and stays theirs. This file is ours: the band's DAW has its front end in `../Trickfilm` (`pipeline/lab/`, the timeline and playback as John sees them), its analysis and offline DSP as tools of `../Consonance`'s `daw/` engine, and here the native pieces a browser cannot give: a real-time CoreAudio engine, Audio Unit, VST3 and CLAP hosting, MIDI device I/O, recording, an offline mix engine. John's decision of 12 and 13 October 2026 (`../robot-release/sessions/2026-10-13/session.md`): "we can take the hard backend stuff from soundcraft as it develops, and have our UI more logic flavored."

## How the fork is kept

- `upstream` is `https://github.com/storytold/soundcraft`; `main` tracks `upstream/main` and never carries our commits. `origin` is John's private repo `git@github-personal:John-Therrien-WA/soundcraft.git`; nothing is published from here.
- **Our delta lives on `wild-animal` and stays small:** the bridge crate and our plugin crate below (and their entries in `Cargo.lock`), this file, one import line in `CLAUDE.md`, and nothing inside upstream's crates unless the same fix goes upstream as a pull request and is carried here only until it lands. Taking upstream's progress is `git fetch upstream`, a rebase of `wild-animal` onto `upstream/main`, then the checks. A conflict inside upstream's crates means our delta grew where it should not.
- The toolchain is `~/.cargo/bin` (rustup, cargo 1.99; not on PATH by default). A build writes `target/` here (6.6 GB in debug) and the registry in `~/.cargo` (`~/.cargo/registry` and `~/.cargo/git` are in the sandbox's write set): builds, the release build of the bridge and the test suites of `soundcraft-dsp`, `soundcraft-bridge` and `wild-animal-plugins` run sandboxed, measured on 17 October 2026. The linker's warning `could not create cache file '.../xcrun_db-...' (errno=Operation not permitted)` on every sandboxed build is harmless, not a failed build. Formatting is `~/.cargo/bin/cargo fmt -p <crate>` (the workspace is edition 2024; a bare `rustfmt --edition 2021` reformats it wrong).
- Inside a sandboxed shell the Audio Unit registry is empty and the VST3 scan crashes (SIGTRAP), so the one suite that scans plugins, `cargo test -p soundcraft-mix`, dies sandboxed every time (`signal: 5, SIGTRAP`) and passes hatched (40 tests): it is sent hatched from the start, and the family's hatch gate knows it as a known sandbox failure and asks for no sandboxed try first. A filtered run of its lib tests (`--lib -- <name>`) that touches no host passes sandboxed. Logic's own instruments are not system Audio Units; nothing but Logic hosts them.

## What upstream gives, as established on 12 October 2026 (0.6.1, commit 6cf896e)

From a read of the code and a run of the whole suite on John's Mac (about 690 tests, all passing, `au-host/tests/system_units.rs` driving Apple's shipped units):

- **Worth taking as they are:** `clap-host`, `vst3-host`, `au-host` (own FFI, in-process, each depending only on `dsp`), `mix` (offline and real-time render with plugin delay compensation), `model` (`Session`, `.scraft` JSON, undo snapshots, automation lanes), `time`, `audio-io`, `midi` (SMF, 960 PPQ), the capture ring in `playback/record.rs`, `engine` (about 469 JSON commands behind one registry), `automation` (MCP).
- **Missing or weak, confirmed in code:** no headless real-time audio (sound plays only through the egui app; the CLI lacks `playback`); no device or buffer-size choice (cpal's default device, a fixed 512 block); no MIDI device input; no recording latency compensation; plugins get no tempo or transport (VST3 hard-coded to 120 BPM in 4/4, CLAP null); all hosting in-process (a bad plugin kills the process); the audio callback hands work to the global rayon pool and allocates, so dropouts under load are expected until that changes, upstream or here.
- **Measured here:** a two-track bounce in 0.06 s with gain and pan to 0.02 dB; Apple's AUDelay inserted and a parameter set by command; 85 AUs, 59 VST3 and 3 CLAP found outside the sandbox; the CLI starts in about 6 ms; the MCP process answers in under a millisecond.

## Our crate: `apps/soundcraft-bridge`

The headless binary the lab talks to. It runs upstream's `engine` (and through it the three plugin hosts) without a window, plays through `playback::Player` on an output device, and speaks upstream's newline-delimited JSON control protocol (`docs/control-protocol.md`) over stdio, and over `127.0.0.1` with `--control <port>` as the app has it. stdout carries only the protocol; the log goes to stderr. Its `README.md` is the protocol reference for the lab's builder: every method with its params and result, the flags, the exit codes, how to spawn it.

- **Methods:** `engine.execute {command, params}` (every engine command), `engine.commands`, `engine.parity`, `session.inspect {detail}`, the `transport.*` commands as methods of their own, `transport.status` (playing, the position in session samples, the device's actual rate, buffer and channels, any output trouble), `devices` (the output devices with their rates and buffer ranges), `bridge.quit`.
- **Flags:** `--device <name>` and `--buffer <frames>`, checked against the device list before anything opens (an unknown device is refused with the list, a buffer outside the device's range is refused, a device that does not take the buffer is refused); `--no-audio` (upstream's silent clock); `--control <port>`. The bridge never falls back to silence on its own: exit 3.
- **The transport glue** is the app's (`ui-egui/src/lib.rs`, `handle_transport`, `start_play`, `stop_play`) without the UI: after every command the engine's transport requests become `Player` calls, and while playing the player's position is read back into `engine.transport`. The dispatch is the bridge's own: the app's control server needs egui, and `automation::Headless` drops transport requests.
- **Tests:** `~/.cargo/bin/cargo test -p soundcraft-bridge` (sandboxed). A child process is driven over stdio through `session.new`, `file.import_audio`, `mix.volume` and `file.bounce_mix`, and two bounces 6 dB apart are checked; the transport runs on the silent clock in process and over stdio; device and buffer choice is checked against synthetic and real device lists. Two tests open the default output and are ignored by default (`-- --ignored` runs them, hatched).
- **The lab** finds the binary at `../soundcraft/target/release/soundcraft-bridge` (or `SOUNDCRAFT_BRIDGE`), built with `~/.cargo/bin/cargo build --release -p soundcraft-bridge`, spawns it with piped stdio, and ends it by closing stdin.

What still needs upstream, or more of the bridge:

- Our changes inside upstream's `crates/playback` (`Player::silent`, the silent clock on request for `--no-audio`, with its test; the clock thread joined when the player drops; `Player::start_error`, the reason a built stream would not start; one shared `Shared` constructor) are small commits of their own, to be filed as a pull request.
- Our changes inside upstream's `crates/dsp` and `crates/mix`, each a commit of its own with its test, to be filed as pull requests: the plugin building blocks (`params`, the `util` smoother, delay line and helpers, `butterworth_qs`) public; `register_external`, a registry for plugins from other crates that `plugins()`, `plugin_info()` and `create()` consult; `Plugin::set_tempo_map`, with `MixEngine` handing the session's tempo map to every plugin on a sync and on a change.
- `xtask/src/main.rs` lists `soundcraft-bridge` in `EXEMPT`, beside upstream's own apps, and gives `wild-animal-plugins` layer 1 in `LAYERS` (the layer of `soundcraft-dsp`, which it builds on), so `cargo xtask layers` checks our plugin crate like upstream's: a dependency on `soundcraft-mix` or anything above fails it.
- Recording (`transport.record` is refused), MIDI device input, tempo and transport for hosted third-party plugins (our own plugins get the tempo map), recording latency compensation, out-of-process plugin hosting, and an audio callback that neither allocates nor hands work to the rayon pool. In the order the lab reaches for them, tempo and transport for hosted plugins comes next.
- The position is the render head (it leads the audible output) and advances in steps of the render quantum, at most 512 session samples; a device running at its own default buffer does not report that size. A device rate unlike the session's is resampled by linear interpolation without a filter. Both are in the crate `README.md`.

## Our plugins: `crates/wild-animal-plugins`

The band's own plugins are written in Rust inside this DAW, in a crate of ours that implements upstream's `Plugin` trait (`crates/dsp/src/lib.rs`), so the engine lists, creates, automates and renders each one like a built-in, and each can be edited and forked here. John's decision of 17 October 2026. Export as CLAP, with clap-wrapper for VST3 and AU, is a thin wrapper over the same code and comes later.

- **The catalogue:** `wa_eq7` ("WA EQ 7": HPF, low shelf, three peaks, high shelf, LPF, RBJ cookbook biquads) and `wa_compressor` ("WA Compressor": feed-forward, stereo-linked, log-domain soft knee, lookahead, sidechain high-pass, limiter mode), forks of upstream's EQ 7-Band and Compressor/Limiter. Each file names the upstream file and commit it was forked from in its first line; upstream's copies stay as they are. They are built from upstream's `Biquad`, `Coeffs`, `params` and `util` through `soundcraft-dsp`'s public API.
- **Registration:** `wild_animal_plugins::register()` adds the catalogue through `soundcraft_dsp::register_external`, after which `plugins()`, `plugin_info()` and `create()` (and with them `engine.plugins`, `mix.insert`, rendering and bouncing) know our ids. The bridge calls it once at the top of `main`; a clash exits 5.
- **Tempo:** a plugin that follows tempo implements `TempoAware` (`set_tempo_map(Arc<TempoMap>)`) and forwards upstream's type-erased `Plugin::set_tempo_map` to it with `forward_tempo_map`. The mixer hands every insert and instrument the session's `TempoMap` when it enters the session and whenever the map changes, so the plugin reads the map itself as shared structure, never only the per-block `Transport.tempo`. Neither fork uses tempo; the handle is for the plugins that follow (the Airwindows rewrites). `tests/tempo_map.rs` checks the path end to end: a `TempoAware` test plugin, registered and inserted by id, receives the session's map through a `MixEngine` render when it enters the session and again when the map changes.
- **Measured, not trusted:** upstream's DSP is agent-written and unchecked, so each plugin comes with checks against the textbook, run with `~/.cargo/bin/cargo test -p wild-animal-plugins` (sandboxed; `-- --nocapture` prints the tables). `src/measure.rs` is the harness for every later plugin: a sine drive, steady-state gain by a least-squares fit, gain-reduction traces, 63 % times. The EQ tests compare the running filter with its analytic response at 24 frequencies for four settings (worst 0.005 dB, at 20 Hz under a 24 dB/oct HPF) and with the cookbook and Butterworth landmarks (within 0.006 dB); flat is bit-transparent and silence after signal decays to exact zero without subnormals. The compressor tests find the gain computer exact on a constant level (0.001 dB) and attack and release reaching 63 % at their settings within one sample; on a tone the detector follows the waveform, so at the default 10 ms attack and 100 ms release the steady reduction sits 0.82, 1.23 and 1.48 dB under the curve at ratio 2, 4 and 10 (under 0.1 dB at 1 and 1000 ms), attack takes 1.68 times its setting at 10 ms and 2.19 times at 1 ms, and a 5 ms lookahead lets a tone step through by 1.12 dB at a 1 ms attack (limiter mode: 1.49 dB over its ceiling). Those three are pinned as measured in two-sided bands (0.80 to 0.86, 1.20 to 1.27 and 1.45 to 1.51 dB; 1.6 to 2.3 times; 0.9 to 1.3 dB), so a regression and a fix of the detector both show in the tests. The textbook bound, a tone within 0.2 dB of the curve, is kept as an ignored test (`tone_follows_the_static_curve`), so `-- --ignored` shows the miss; the causes are upstream's, listed under [Findings against upstream, not filed](#findings-against-upstream-not-filed).

## Findings against upstream, not filed

Measured with our plugins' tests and their harness (`crates/wild-animal-plugins`), against upstream's code as carried on `wild-animal`. Each is a pull request or an issue for `storytold/soundcraft` once John files it; our forks keep upstream's behaviour until then, pinned as measured. Line numbers are the `wild-animal` tree's.

- **The compressor's gain computer runs on the instantaneous sample.** `crates/dsp/src/plugins/dynamics.rs:120` (`gain_for`): the static curve is applied to each sample's level and the ballistics smooth the result after it. Textbook: Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss 2012, "Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design: A Tutorial and Analysis", JAES 60(6), which puts a level detector (peak or RMS, with its own attack and release) before the gain computer. Measured on a 1 kHz tone at threshold -20 dBFS, 10 ms attack, 100 ms release: the steady reduction sits 0.82, 1.23 and 1.48 dB under the static curve at ratio 2, 4 and 10, and attack reaches 63 % at 1.68 times its setting (10 ms) and 2.19 times (1 ms). Fix: a peak detector with hold, or the decoupled smooth detector of the paper, in front of the gain computer.
- **Lookahead is a pure delay.** `crates/dsp/src/plugins/dynamics.rs:185`: the audio is read `la + 1` samples back while the gain moves at its own attack, with nothing tying the attack to the lookahead window. Measured on a tone step from -40 to -10 dBFS (threshold -20, ratio 4, attack 1 ms): the first 5 ms pass 1.12 dB over the steady level with 5 ms lookahead, and the limiter mode 1.49 dB over its ceiling. Fix: reach the target reduction across the lookahead window (ramp the gain over the window, or take the window's peak as the detector's input).
- **`time_coef` is computed in f32.** `crates/dsp/src/util.rs:29`: `(-1.0 / n).exp()` in single precision for a one-pole time constant of `n` samples. Measured: a 1000 ms release reaches 63 % at 998.61 ms at 48 kHz. Fix: compute the coefficient in f64 and store it as f32.
- **An insert's parameters arrive after it is prepared.** `crates/mix/src/lib.rs:1675` (`apply_params`, line 1650 at upstream's c2b7efd) runs at the first block, after `make` (line 401; 399 at c2b7efd) has prepared the instance through `create_instance` (line 147) at its defaults, so the plugin's smoothers ramp from the default to the session's value. Measured: the first 2 ms of a bounce through a gain set to -6 dB output sit at -0.058 dB. Fix: apply the insert's values before `prepare` (or reset the smoothers to their targets after the first `apply_params`).
